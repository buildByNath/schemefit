Himachal Pradesh

Unemployment Allowance Scheme
Allowance Employment exchange Unemployed youth
State: Himachal Pradesh
Ministry: Labour and Employment
Allowance: Rs 1,000 to Rs 1,500 per month
Duration: Up to 2 years
Age: 20 to 35 years
Processing: 45 days

Details

The Department of Labour and Employment, Government of Himachal Pradesh introduced the Unemployment Allowance Scheme to provide allowance to eligible educated unemployed youth of Himachal Pradesh, so they can sustain themselves for a certain period.

The scheme aims to provide:

  1. Financial support: a monthly unemployment allowance to educated unemployed youth, offering a basic financial support system.
  2. Empowerment of educated youth: targeted support while candidates search for employment opportunities.
  3. Stability in lives: a regular income to help meet basic needs during the job search.
  4. Inclusive approach: a higher amount of assistance for physically challenged individuals.
  5. Age-appropriate eligibility: focused on the 20 to 35 year age group.

Benefits

Allowance is payable for a maximum of two years, subject to fulfilling eligibility criteria and timely submission of attested marksheets and a self-declaration (Form C).

Eligibility

An educated unemployed applicant is eligible for the grant of Unemployment Allowance if they fulfil the following criteria:

Exclusions

The following categories of persons are not eligible to receive the Unemployment Allowance:

  • Government, PSU, local body, or quasi-government employees dismissed from service.
  • Persons imprisoned for a period of 48 hours or more.
  • Persons whose family income was Rs 2,00,000 or more in the year preceding application.
  • Persons who are not bonafide Himachalis.
  • Persons below 20 years or above 35 years of age.

Documents required

  • Attested copy of Employment Registration Card (X-10)
  • Attested copy of a valid family income certificate, not more than 6 months old
  • Income certificate stating the applicant is not employed in government or PSU roles
  • Self-certified Declaration Form C, declaring unemployment
  • Attested copy of Bonafide Himachali Certificate
  • Bank details verified by the bank branch officer, IFSC code mandatory
  • Attested copies of 10th or 12th certificate and age proof
  • Attested copy of Aadhaar card

Frequently asked questions

Who is eligible to apply for the Unemployment Allowance Scheme in Himachal Pradesh?
Eligible applicants must be bonafide Himachali residents, aged 20 to 35, with minimum 10+2 qualification, registered with an Employment Exchange in HP for at least 1 year, unemployed, and with an annual family income below Rs 2 lakh.
Is there an online application process for the scheme?
Yes. Applications can be submitted online via the Department of Labour and Employment website. After filling the form, a printout must be taken and submitted with attested documents to the District or Sub-Employment Exchange Office.
What documents are required for the application?
Required documents include an attested Employment Registration Card, income certificate not older than 6 months, self-certified Declaration Form C, Bonafide Himachali Certificate, educational certificates, Aadhaar card, and verified bank details with IFSC code.
Where should the application and documents be submitted?
The application form along with all attached documents must be submitted to the District or Sub-Employment Exchange Office in the applicant's district.
How long does the selection process take?
The Employment Exchange Office scrutinises the application and communicates the decision within 45 days of submission, notifying applicants by SMS.
Can physically challenged individuals apply for the scheme?
Yes. Physically challenged persons with minimum 50% permanent disability recorded in their Employment Exchange record (X-I) can apply and receive Rs 1,500 per month, higher than the general category amount of Rs 1,000.
